# Manual-IJET
A Manual Archipelago implementation of Indiana Jones and the Emperor's Tomb. Requires a fully completed save as each individual chapter are considered unlocks.
# Items:
- Equipment (find the Equipment unlock item to use your Whip, Tools, Guns, Potions, etc. Basically everything in Indy's satchel.)
- Chapter Unlocks (You start in Ceylon: The Gates of the Lost City but could unlock an Istanbul level in it.)
- Demo Charges / Other Objective-related (some levels require certain items to be able to progress in that level)

# Locations:
- All ammo/equipment pickups in each chapter
- Objective items
- Fountain-sanity (use the fountain for the check - requires Canteen unlock)
- Complete all previous chapters to unlock the final chapter in the level

# To note:
- Some of the one-off items do not have equipment unlocks associated with them. If you find a shovel or a Panzerschreck, have fun. I debated adding an improvised items unlock but did not.
- The final chapter of each level is gated behind completing all the previous chapters of the Level. So to unlock the final chapter of Ceylon, you need to complete the previous 8 chapters to get the unlock item. The final level, The Netherworld, is unlocked after completing every other final chapter.
- I did my best trying to include everything but may have missed a pickup here and there. Let me know if you try it.
- Also, I tried my best with location names and sorting for the manual client, but if anything is not understandable let me know
# Setup:
- If you do not have a fully completed save file, copy "Indy.ses" to ```Indiana Jones and the Emperors Tomb/GameData/indy/saves```.
- The game does not have a native Windowed Mode and alt-tabbing will crash the game, which can make playing a manual challenging. Included are ```d3d8.dll```, ```dxwrapper.dll```, and ```dxwrapper.ini```. Copy those into ```Indiana Jones and the Emperors Tomb/GameData/bin``` and the game will launch in Windowed Mode.
